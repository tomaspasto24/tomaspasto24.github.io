About me

Systems Engineer, recently graduated

Hello! I'm Tomás Silva Pastorini, a Systems Engineer from the Catholic University of Uruguay (UCU), where I graduated in December 2025. I work as a fullstack developer at GlobalUY, building an internal agricultural planning module for INALE (Instituto Nacional de la Leche). I am also a teaching assistant for ANDIS at UCU.

My stack ranges from frontend (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Angular, Next.js) to backend (Express, NestJS, PHP, Ruby on Rails) and tools such as Docker, Git, AWS, and Drupal.

GlobalUY · INALE

Agricultural planning and prediction module

The planning module builds the diet and the use of pastures and reserves for a dairy farm throughout the year. A solver looks for the lowest cost while meeting nutritional requirements.

VM (Vaca Masa) plans the adult herd in production (milking), per hectare of vaca masa. Recría uses the same approach for growing animals — lots, weight gain, and their own requirements — in a separate flow.
